Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the UptoZishu Developer Console ("Console", "we", "us") collects from developers, how we use it, who can see it and the choices you have. The Console is where developers upload and manage apps, buy promotion plans and manage their developer profile for the UptoZishu store. By creating an account or using the Console you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, name, username and sign-in method (email and password, or Google). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • Developer profile: details you choose to add, such as organization name, tagline, about text, location, logo, cover image, website and social links. The public parts of this profile are shown on your developer page in the store.
  • App content: app names, descriptions, icons, screenshots, banners, download links, categories, versions and release notes that you upload, and the changes you submit for approval.
  • Plan and payment records: when you request a Sponsored, Featured, Verified badge or Developer Pro plan we collect the plan, the app(s) chosen, duration and amount, your name, phone number and contact email, the UPI transaction (UTR) number, payment date, the payment screenshot you upload and any note you write. We pay close attention to what we collect here: we never ask for your UPI PIN, card number, bank password or OTP.
  • Activity on the platform: reviews and your replies to them, user feedback sent about your apps and what you do with it (reviewed or deleted), notifications, support tickets, ownership (claim) requests and the proof you submit with them, and reports made about your apps.
  • Moderation records: decisions our team makes on your apps, updates, profile, plan requests and account (approved, rejected, suspended, verified), the reason given, and the date.
  • App statistics: counts such as views, downloads and ratings for your apps.
  • On your device: drafts, theme, notification preferences and small display settings (for example, that you closed a promotion banner) are saved in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To publish your apps and developer page in the UptoZishu store after our team has reviewed them.
  • To show you analytics, reviews, feedback and notifications about your apps.
  • To check plan requests, confirm payments, activate and end plans, and show badges and placements in the store.
  • To prevent fraud, fake payments, spam and abuse, and to keep accounting and dispute records.
  • To review ownership claims, handle reports and moderate content, including removing apps or suspending accounts that break our rules.
  • To send you notifications and service emails about your account, apps, plan decisions, tickets and important changes.

3. What is public and who can see what

Public: your username, developer name, public profile details, your Verified status, your published apps (descriptions, screenshots, ratings) and your replies to reviews. Apps and developers in a paid plan are labelled in the store (for example in the Sponsored or Featured list).

Private: your email address, account details, support tickets, notifications, and all plan and payment records are private. They are visible only to you and to the UptoZishu administrators who review them. Your email is not shown publicly unless you add it to your public profile.

4. Service providers

We rely on trusted providers to run the Console: Google Firebase (authentication and database), Google Apps Script and Gmail (sending service emails), imgbb (hosting of images you upload, such as icons, screenshots, profile images and payment screenshots) and GitHub Pages (hosting of the website). These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. Images you upload are stored on imgbb and can be opened by anyone who has the exact link, so please do not put sensitive details such as full card or bank numbers in a payment screenshot; hide anything that is not needed to show the transaction.

5. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose information only when required by law, when a competent authority asks for it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the service, for example to investigate payment fraud.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account, profile and app data is kept while your account is active.
  • Plan and payment records are kept for as long as needed for accounting, dispute handling and fraud prevention, even if you later delete your account.
  • Moderation and suspension records may be kept to protect the store from repeat abuse.
  • Backups and provider logs may keep copies for a short time after deletion.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings → Data and privacy → Download my data gives you a copy of your account, profile and apps.
  • Edit: you can edit your profile and apps at any time from the Console. Some changes go to our team for approval before they show in the store.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account removes your account, apps, public profile, username and notifications from our database. Records we must keep under section 6, and images already on imgbb, may remain as described there.
  • Local data: Settings → Clear local data removes drafts and preferences saved on your device.
  • Questions or complaints: email us at the address below and we will reply within a reasonable time. You may also use any rights you have under the laws that apply to you.

8. Security

We use Firebase security rules and HTTPS to protect your data, and only administrators can open plan and payment records. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password, sign out on shared devices and never share your sign-in details. If you believe your account was accessed without your permission, contact us at once.

9. Children

The Console is for people who are at least 18 years old, or who have the permission of a parent or guardian and are old enough to enter into a binding agreement where they live. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Console grows, for example when we add features. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If you keep using the Console after a change, you accept the updated policy.
