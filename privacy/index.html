Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the UptoZishu Developer Console ("Console", "we", "us") collects from developers, how we use it and the choices you have. The Console is where developers upload and manage apps that appear on the UptoZishu store.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, name, username and sign-in method (email and password, or Google). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • Developer profile: details you choose to add, such as organization name, tagline, about text, location, logo, cover image, website and social links. The public parts of this profile are shown on your developer page on the store.
  • App content: app names, descriptions, icons, screenshots, download links, categories, versions and release notes that you upload.
  • Activity on the platform: reviews and your replies to them, notifications, ownership (claim) requests and the proof you submit with them, and reports made about your apps.
  • App statistics: counts such as views, downloads and ratings for your apps.
  • On your device: drafts, an activity log, theme and notification preferences are saved in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To publish your apps and developer page on the UptoZishu store.
  • To show you analytics, reviews and notifications about your apps.
  • To review ownership claims, handle reports and moderate content, including suspending apps that break our rules.
  • To contact you about your account, your apps or important service changes.

3. What is public

Your username, developer name, public profile details, and your published apps (including their descriptions, screenshots, ratings and your replies to reviews) are visible to anyone using the store. Your email address and account details are private and are not shown publicly unless you add an email to your public profile.

4. Service providers

We rely on trusted providers to run the Console: Google Firebase (authentication and database), imgbb (hosting of images you upload, such as icons, screenshots and profile images) and GitHub Pages (hosting of the website). These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. Files you upload as images are stored on imgbb and are reachable by their link.

5. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose information only when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the service.

6. Data retention and your choices

  • Access and export: Settings → Data and privacy → Download my data gives you a copy of your account, profile and apps.
  • Edit: you can edit your profile and apps at any time from the Console.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, apps, public profile, username and notifications from our database. Images already uploaded to imgbb may remain available at their links until they expire or are removed by the provider.
  • Local data: Settings → Clear local data removes drafts and preferences saved on your device.

7. Security

We use Firebase security rules and HTTPS to protect your data. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices.

8. Children

The Console is intended for people who are old enough to enter into a binding agreement where they live. It is not directed at young children, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If you continue to use the Console after a change, you accept the updated policy.
